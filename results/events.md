## How the model works

Projected points = chance of playing × the sum over the gameweek's fixtures of the expected points from each scoring event, given the player gets on. A double gameweek sums two fixtures, a blank has none and projects 0. It reads only the leak-proof history view, and FPL's scoring rules come from `lib/analysis/fpl-scoring.ts` (the 2022-23 to 2024-25 rules; 2025-26's defensive contribution points are not in them yet).

- **Chance of playing**: step 3's logistic model, unchanged.
- **Minutes**: a second logistic gives the chance of 60+ minutes once on, from the same playing-time features plus position. Expected minutes mix the average 60+ and shorter appearance.
- **Goals**: recent xG per 90 (last 6 appearances, blended with last season, then the league), times finishing (goals per xG over both seasons, shrunk toward 1), times how leaky the opponent's defence is and home or away.
- **Assists**: xA per 90 the same way, without finishing.
- **Team strength**: each team's xG for and against per match, blended with 6 matches of its own last season (promoted sides start from last season's bottom three). A fixture's expected goals are league average × attack × the opponent's defence × home or away.
- **Clean sheet**: only after 60+ minutes, chance of 0 goals against while on the pitch.
- **Goals conceded** (keepers and defenders): −1 per 2, worked out exactly on the Poisson distribution, not from the mean.
- **Saves** (keepers): the keeper's own saves per 90, scaled by the opponent's attack; 1 point per 3, again exact on the Poisson.
- **Bonus**: a linear model of bonus on the match's events, fitted on what actually happened, then fed the expected events. Capped at 0 to 3.
- **Appearance**: 2 points for 60+ minutes, 1 for less.
- **Other**: the average of what the events leave out (cards, own goals, penalties), per position.

**The Poisson assumption.** Goals, assists, goals conceded and saves are each treated as Poisson: independent events at a steady rate for the match, so the chance of none is e^(−mean). That is the standard first model for football counts. It is not quite right. Real goals clump a little, so Poisson overstates clean sheets (corrected by the clean sheet scale below) and slightly understates the chance of a brace or a hat-trick.

## What the fitted numbers say

The final numbers, fitted on both tune seasons together and saved in `lib/analysis/event-weights.ts`.

- **Chance of playing** is step 3's model, refitted in each fold on the same features. It is not repeated here; see [projection.md](projection.md).
- **60 minutes or more.** Once on the pitch, a regular who started the last 3 reaches 60 minutes about this often: keeper 100%, defender 96%, midfielder 92%, forward 90%. A midfielder who came off the bench in each of the last 3, about 23%. A 60+ appearance averages 85 minutes, a shorter one 22.
- **Finishing.** A player's goals per xG is blended with 20 xG of "exactly on xG". So a player who has scored 15 from 10 xG is rated 17% above their xG, not 50%: most over- or under-performance is luck.
- **FPL credits 1.40 assists per xA.** FPL's assist rules are looser than an Opta assist (a rebound or a won penalty can count), so raw xA undercounts. Goals come out at 0.96 per finishing-adjusted xG, saves at 1.01 per expected save, goals conceded at 1.04 per xG against.
- **Clean sheets are rarer than Poisson says.** Goals against are scaled by 1.10 inside the clean sheet chance, so that it predicts as many clean sheets as were really kept. Real goals clump (one goal makes the next more likely), and own goals and some penalties sit outside xG.
- **Bonus**, the biggest weights: per goal +1.23, clean sheet, keeper or defender +0.57, per assist +0.49 bonus points each.
- **Other points** (cards, own goals, penalties, which the scoring events above leave out) average keeper -0.02, defender -0.19, midfielder -0.16, forward -0.12 per appearance.

## How it's measured

- A model predicting gameweek N sees only rows from earlier gameweeks and earlier seasons. It is handed the player, price and fixtures for N, never the result.
- **MAE / RMSE, played**: over player-gameweeks with minutes > 0. **Everyone**: every player whose team had a fixture, including those who didn't play.
- **Spearman, by position**: rank correlation of projected vs actual within each position in each gameweek, averaged over those groups. It covers everyone with a fixture, so it rewards knowing who will play as well as who will score. Ties count: a model that gives unused players exactly 0 matches their actual 0s and gains here. **Spearman, played** is the same over players who got on, so it measures who scores without who plays.
- **Top-5 hit**: average actual points of the 5 highest-projected players in a gameweek.
- **Captain regret**: best actual score among the top 20 projected, minus the actual score of the top projected player.
- Ties in a projection are never broken by player id: tied players share the tied group's average. So the constant's top-5 is the average player, and its Spearman only reflects double gameweeks (two fixtures project 4, one projects 2).
- A double gameweek is one prediction with both fixtures' points summed.

## Baselines

- **FPL's xP (leaky)**: Fpl's own ep_this from vaastav's archive. may have seen team news or the result.
- **Form (30 days)**: Fpl's definition: mean points per club match kicked off in the 30 days before the gameweek, 0 if none.
- **Points per game**: Fpl's definition: points this season over matches with minutes, 0 if none.
- **Last 3 gameweeks**: Mean points per match over the player's last 3 gameweeks with a fixture, benched ones included.
- **Constant 2**: 2 points per fixture for everyone: the simplest possible guess.
