## How the model works

Projected points = chance of playing × expected points if playing, summed over the gameweek's fixtures (a double gameweek has two, a blank has none and projects 0). It reads only the leak-proof history view.

- **Chance of playing** is a logistic regression on the share of the last 3 club matches started, the share with any minutes, the share of this season's matches with minutes, and price.
- **Points if playing** is a linear blend of a base per position; points per appearance this season, over the last 30 days and over the last 3 gameweeks; expected goals and assists per 90 over the last 6 appearances, turned into FPL points over the player's usual minutes; price; fixture difficulty (separately for defensive and attacking players); and home or away.
- **Last season as a prior.** Every rate is blended with 4 appearances (or 90s) of prior evidence: the player's own numbers from last season, found by FPL's stable player code, or the league average for their position if they are new. So in gameweek 1 a proven scorer starts from last season, not from a flat 2. Matched by code: 2023-24: 8086 of 11086 player-gameweeks with minutes (73%); 2024-25: 8686 of 11431 player-gameweeks with minutes (76%).
- The fit uses one example per player per fixture: all of them for chance of playing, those with minutes for points if playing. Points if playing uses a ridge penalty chosen by cross-validation; chance of playing a light fixed one.

## What the weights say

The final weights, fitted on both tune seasons together. These are the numbers saved in `lib/analysis/projection-weights.ts`.

- **Who plays comes first.** A regular who started the last 3 and played every match so far is given about 96% to play; one benched for the last 3 who has played half the season, about 8%.
- **Once on the pitch, a typical player scores** about: keeper 3.25, defender 2.43, midfielder 2.75, forward 3.17 points.
- **The biggest scoring signals** are "Points per appearance, this season" and "Price, per £1m": a typical difference between two players in each moves the projection by 0.31 and 0.30 points.
- **Fixtures**: each step of difficulty costs a keeper or defender 0.41 and costs a midfielder or forward 0.30; home is worth +0.11.
- Form, the last 3 gameweeks and the season average all measure points per appearance, so they share credit between them. Read them together, not one by one.

## How it's measured

- A model predicting gameweek N sees only rows from earlier gameweeks and earlier seasons. It is handed the player, price and fixtures for N, never the result.
- **MAE / RMSE, played**: over player-gameweeks with minutes > 0. **Everyone**: every player whose team had a fixture, including those who didn't play.
- **Spearman, by position**: rank correlation of projected vs actual within each position in each gameweek, averaged over those groups. It covers everyone with a fixture, so it rewards knowing who will play as well as who will score. Ties count: a model that gives unused players exactly 0 matches their actual 0s and gains here. **Spearman, played** is the same over players who got on, so it measures who scores without who plays.
- **Top-5 hit**: average actual points of the 5 highest-projected players in a gameweek.
- **Captain regret**: best actual score among the top 20 projected, minus the actual score of the top projected player.
- Ties in a projection are never broken by player id: tied players share the tied group's average. So the constant's top-5 is the average player, and its Spearman only reflects double gameweeks (two fixtures project 4, one projects 2).
- A double gameweek is one prediction with both fixtures' points summed.

## Baselines

- **FPL's xP (leaky)**: Fpl's own ep_this from vaastav's archive. may have seen team news or the result.
- **Form (30 days)**: Fpl's definition: mean points per club match kicked off in the 30 days before the gameweek, 0 if none.
- **Points per game**: Fpl's definition: points this season over matches with minutes, 0 if none.
- **Last 3 gameweeks**: Mean points per match over the player's last 3 gameweeks with a fixture, benched ones included.
- **Constant 2**: 2 points per fixture for everyone: the simplest possible guess.
