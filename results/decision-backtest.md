# Decision backtest

Generated by `npm run backtest:register` and then, once, `npm run backtest`. Don't edit by hand.

## Pre-registered rule

Written before any season was played. Nothing in the app or the models is tuned on these results; the app is not changed by this run.

**What is played.** Whole seasons, deadline by deadline, with FPL's real results: a 15-man squad, £100.0m, three per club, free transfers rolling over (at most 5, one going into gameweek 2), -4 hits, auto-subs, the captain's points doubled and the vice-captain's if the captain doesn't play, real price changes, and FPL's selling price (half of any rise, rounded down). No chips. Each deadline, a policy sees only data from before that gameweek.

**Seasons and model weights.**

- **2023-24**: out of fold: both models and the blend share fitted on 2024-25 only, as in results/ensemble.md
- **2024-25**: out of fold: both models and the blend share fitted on 2023-24 only, as in results/ensemble.md
- **2025-26**: run once with the frozen weights (both models and the blend share 0.4892), the app's own; nothing is tuned on it

**Start squads.** 20 per season, the same for every policy, built at gameweek 1 from last season's data only: squad 1 is the best guess (the most FPL points last season that fits the budget, the squad shape and three per club, picked greedily while the rest can still be afforded at the cheapest prices); squads 2 to 20 multiply each player's points last season by e^(0.4 z), z a seeded normal draw, and pick the same way. Players new to the league count 0.

**Policies.** Every policy picks its eleven, bench order, captain and vice-captain with the app's own lineup and captaincy code, on its own numbers.

1. **No transfers**: the start squad all season; eleven and captain by the model.
2. **Form greedy**: the app's one-week transfer logic, but every number is FPL's form (points per match over the last 30 days, times the week's fixtures). Eleven and captain by form too.
3. **Onze one-week**: the app's one-week transfer logic on the model's numbers, as the app runs it now.
4. **Onze planner**: the app's 5-week planner as it is now (K = 40, each week weighted 0.85 of the one before, the same hit rules), replanned every deadline, playing only that week's part.

**Headline, deciding.** Onze planner minus Onze one-week in season points (after hits), paired on the same start squads and gameweeks, averaged over the start squads and then over the three seasons. The 95% interval resamples whole gameweeks within each season (2000 resamples, seed 2026), so it carries the luck of the weeks that every squad shares. **If the planner is not clearly worse (the interval's top is not below 0), it becomes the app's single source of this week's advice.** Otherwise the one-week logic stays.

**Also reported, not deciding.** Each Onze policy against no transfers and against form greedy, the same way; season totals for every policy; each season on its own; and two example seasons in plain words.

**Known before running.**

- The archive has no team news, so the model sees injuries only through missing minutes, as every policy does. A player who has left the league is shown as unavailable from the gameweek after his last row.
- The fixture list is read as it was finally played, so postponed matches show up in their final gameweek.
- There are no mini-leagues, so the no-hits-when-leading rule never applies.
- Three seasons is few. The interval carries week-to-week luck within those seasons, not how different another season might be.

## Verdict

**The planner is not clearly worse, so by the rule it becomes the app's single source of this week's advice.** Onze planner minus Onze one-week: +28.8 points a season [-16.3, +72.8]. It is not clearly better either: the interval includes 0. Nothing in the app is changed by this run.

## Two seasons in plain words

**2024-25, squad #3.** Following Onze's planner from squad #3, you would have finished 2024-25 with 2236 points, against 2093 following its one-week advice, 1217 making no transfers at all and 1850 chasing form. That's 143 points more than the one-week advice and 386 points more than chasing form.

**2025-26, the best-guess squad (#1).** Following Onze's planner from the best-guess squad (#1), you would have finished 2025-26 with 1898 points, against 1975 following its one-week advice, 1195 making no transfers at all and 1911 chasing form. That's 77 points fewer than the one-week advice and 13 points fewer than chasing form.
