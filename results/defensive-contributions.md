# Defensive contributions

Generated by `npm run eval:defensive`. Don't edit by hand.

2026-27 gameweeks 1 to 10: every appearance by a defender, midfielder or forward (2883 in all). 2025-26 is read only as last season's history for the priors; nothing is fitted or scored on it. Data: vaastav/Fantasy-Premier-League at `d7b05b5` and FPL's API.

## The rule term

From 2025-26, FPL gives 2 points for 10 defensive contributions by a defender, or 12 by a midfielder or forward (recoveries count for them). The step 3 model was fitted on seasons without these points, so they are taken out of every past total it reads and added back on top as a rule:

- **Rate**: the player's defensive contributions per 90 over his last 6 appearances, blended with last season's per 90 (by FPL's stable player code) and then the league's for his position.
- **Chance**: Poisson at that rate, worked out for the minutes of each of his recent appearances and averaged, so a full game and a cameo keep their very different chances.
- **Points if he plays**: chance × 2, for each fixture. The projection multiplies it by the chance of playing like the rest of the points.

## Why it runs low

- **Uncertain rates.** Reaching 10 or 12 is in the tail of the distribution. A player's true rate is uncertain, and one best-guess rate is too sure in both directions: in the calibration table, low chances come true more often than given and the few high ones less often. Shrinking toward the league average steadies the rate but pulls the tail in further.
- **Clumping.** Some matches (a side pinned back all game) pile up defensive actions, while Poisson assumes a steady rate within a match.
- Fixing it would mean fitting a correction, and the only seasons with these points are the locked test season and the live one being checked here. So it is left as a known, stated underestimate: worth about 0.039 points per appearance.
