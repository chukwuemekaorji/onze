## Decision rule

An ensemble ships only if, against the two-part model alone on the same resampled gameweeks (paired bootstrap, 95% interval), it is **clearly better on at least one** of top-5 hit, haul share in the top 20 or captain regret (the interval for the difference excludes 0 in its favour) **and clearly worse on none** of them (no interval excluding 0 against it). Otherwise the two-part model stays and the negative result is recorded.

- A separate w per position replaces the single w only if it passes the same test against the single-w blend.
- Routing's captain is the two-part model's own pick, so its captain regret is the two-part model's exactly; its case rests on top-5 and haul share.
- If both ensembles pass, both are recorded as candidates and nothing is chosen here. Wiring one into the app is a separate step.

This rule was written into the script that generates this file before the first ensemble run, and the verdict below is computed from it, not written by hand.

## The blend weights

w is the two-part model's share: 1 is the two-part model alone, 0 the event model alone. In each fold both models are fitted on the training season, and w is the least squares weight over every player with a fixture in that same season, kept to [0, 1]. The held season's results never touch the models or w.

## How it's measured

- A model predicting gameweek N sees only rows from earlier gameweeks and earlier seasons. It is handed the player, price and fixtures for N, never the result.
- **MAE / RMSE, played**: over player-gameweeks with minutes > 0. **Everyone**: every player whose team had a fixture, including those who didn't play.
- **Spearman, by position**: rank correlation of projected vs actual within each position in each gameweek, averaged over those groups. It covers everyone with a fixture, so it rewards knowing who will play as well as who will score. Ties count: a model that gives unused players exactly 0 matches their actual 0s and gains here. **Spearman, played** is the same over players who got on, so it measures who scores without who plays.
- **Top-5 hit**: average actual points of the 5 highest-projected players in a gameweek.
- **Captain regret**: best actual score among the top 20 projected, minus the actual score of the top projected player.
- Ties in a projection are never broken by player id: tied players share the tied group's average. So the constant's top-5 is the average player, and its Spearman only reflects double gameweeks (two fixtures project 4, one projects 2).
- A double gameweek is one prediction with both fixtures' points summed.
- **Haul share in the top 20**: the share of each gameweek's 10+ point scores that the model had on its 20-player shortlist, as in [events.md](events.md).
- **Routing**: top-5 and haul share are read from the event model's ranking (the transfer shortlist), captain regret and captain points from the two-part model's (the armband).
